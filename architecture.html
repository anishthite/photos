Add-a-photo flow

  1. On the site: open /upload, drag files / paste from clipboard / paste a link → /api/upload or /api/import-url writes to R2 and updates the index. No terminal needed.
  2. From iPhone: a Shortcut POSTs {"url":…} to /api/import-url; your 30-day login cookie authenticates it.
  3. From the CLI: scripts/add_photos.sh ~/Desktop/vacation → copies in, rebuilds index, uploads to R2, redeploys.

Security: private R2 (no public URL) · password gate on every route · HMAC-signed cookies · path traversal rejected · Range requests for video scrubbing. Source: github.com/anishthite/photos (code only; photos gitignored). Status: pages.dev live + secured · custom domain pending one DNS record.
